📜 GOVERNANCE & SHARIAH CHARTER

Terms & Transparency Charter

Guiding principles for Zakat eligibility, 100% Tamleek transfer, and vendor audit transparency.

1. Islamic Jurisprudence & Zakat Compliance

All contributions designated for Zakat or Sadaqah Wajibah are segregated and disbursed exclusively according to Quranic Shariah criteria:

  • Eligible recipients are verified as Fuqara (impoverished) or Masakeen (destitute) with no source of income or assets exceeding the Nisab threshold.
  • Principle of Tamleek (Complete Ownership Transfer): Full and unconditional physical ownership of household kits and wedding goods is transferred directly to the bride or her legal guardian.
  • Zero administrative deduction is taken from designated Zakat contributions; 100% goes into the procurement of physical items.

2. Beneficiary Screening & Case Verification

Rigorous Multi-Tier Audit: Every case undergoes direct home inspection by our verified female field coordinators, CNIC verification via NADRA records, and local community vetting.

Cases are prioritized based on:

  • Orphan brides with deceased fathers or single-mother households.
  • Families with disabled breadwinners or extreme medical debts.
  • Households facing severe debt traps due to impending marriage costs.

3. Transparency & Public Invoicing

MarryWithDignity ensures that donors have access to itemized procurement receipts:

  • Wholesale purchase invoices for all items (cookware, sewing machines, bedding, etc.) are matched with anonymized Case IDs.
  • Donors can request itemized vendor receipts for their sponsored kit at any time.

4. Refund & Donation Policy

Due to the immediate allocation of funds toward wholesale inventory procurement, donations are non-refundable once goods are purchased or allocated to a verified case. If a particular wedding case is canceled or postponed indefinitely, the funds are promptly re-allocated to the next urgent verified orphan case.

5. Donor Inquiries & Audit Requests

For audit requests, verification queries, or dedicated sponsorships in memory of deceased relatives, please contact our transparency team at charter@marrywithdignity.org or WhatsApp +92 344 2001535.
