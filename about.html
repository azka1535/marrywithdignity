✨ ABOUT MARRYWITHDIGNITY

Transforming Lives with Honor & Dignity

We exist to lift the crushing debt of marriage expenses from impoverished and orphan families across Pakistan, fulfilling a noble Sunnah with 100% field transparency.

ہمارا مشن: مستحق اور یتیم بچیوں کے لیے ضروری گھریلو سامان اور جہیز کے بھاری قرضوں سے مکمل نجات، بغیر کسی تشہیر اور 100% شفافیت کے ساتھ۔

The Dowry Crisis in Pakistan

In many destitute communities across Pakistan, the societal demand for dowry (Jaheez) turns a joyous occasion into a nightmare. Impoverished daily-wage earners and widowed mothers take loans from predatory local money-lenders at extortionate rates just to buy basic kitchen utensils, bedding, and clothes.

Consequently, deserving daughters remain unmarried for years or enter marital life burdened by family debt. MarryWithDignity was founded to break this cycle of debt and humiliation.

Our Radical Commitment to Dignity

Unlike traditional charity photo-ops, MarryWithDignity guarantees Sad-Faisad Izzat-e-Nafs (Zero Public Exposure). We believe that charity given at the cost of a sister's dignity is not true charity.

We never publish faces, home video tours, or humiliating distribution pictures on social media. Instead, donors receive itemized vendor purchase receipts and verified Case IDs for complete audit accountability.

🏛️ CORE FOUNDATIONS

The 4 Pillars of MarryWithDignity

📦

1. 100% Tamleek Ownership

Every single item (cookware sets, dinnerware, Singer sewing machines, pedestal fans, and bridal bedding) is handed over with complete and unconditional ownership to the bride or guardian, fully satisfying Islamic Zakat jurisprudence.

🕵️‍♀️

2. Female-Led Verification Teams

All home inspections and poverty assessments are conducted by our trained female field coordinators in privacy, ensuring thorough validation without compromising cultural modesty.

⚡

3. 0% Admin Deductions from Zakat

100% of your designated Zakat or Sadaqah funds are spent directly on wholesale market item procurement. All operational and verification costs are covered by private benefactors.

📄

4. Itemized Public Receipt Logs

Every single rupee is accounted for. We preserve wholesale vendor invoices matching each Case ID so donors have complete peace of mind that their amanaat reach the right hands.
